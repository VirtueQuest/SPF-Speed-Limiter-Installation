Traffic Police Statutory Enforcement Deadlines

The Singapore Police Force (SPF) requires all diesel, petrol, and electric lorries with a Maximum Laden Weight (MLW) between 3,501kg and 12,000kg to be retrofitted with a speed limiter restricted to 60km/h. Check your specific timeline below:

Lorry Registration Date Maximum Laden Weight (MLW) Enforcement Deadline
Registered Before 1 Jan 2018 5,001kg – 12,000kg Passed (1 Jan 2026)
Registered Before 1 Jan 2018 3,501kg – 5,000kg (e.g. 10ft/14ft) 1 July 2026 (Upcoming)
Registered On/After 1 Jan 2018 5,001kg – 12,000kg 1 January 2027
Registered On/After 1 Jan 2018 3,501kg – 5,000kg 1 July 2027
